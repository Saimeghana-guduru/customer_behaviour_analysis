# customer_behaviour_analysis
Customer Behaviour Analysis project using data analytics techniques including data preprocessing, exploratory data analysis, and interactive visualization through Power BI dashboards to derive business insights.

Project Overview
This project analyzes customer shopping behavior to understand purchasing patterns, customer demographics, product performance, and factors influencing revenue. The analysis helps identify key trends that businesses can use to improve marketing strategies and customer satisfaction.

Objectives
.Analyze customer demographics and their contribution to revenue
.Identify top-performing products based on sales and ratings
.Understand the impact of discounts on customer purchases
.Evaluate customer satisfaction with subscription offers
.Compare revenue generated from different shipping methods

Dataset Information
.The dataset contains customer shopping information including demographics, product details, ratings, shipping types, and discount usage.

Key details:
.Total Customers: 3.9k
.Features include: Age, Gender, Product Category, Rating, Shipping Type, Discount Applied, Subscription Status, etc.

Tools and Technologies Used
.Power BI – Data visualization and dashboard creation
.Python – Data analysis
.Pandas, NumPy – Data preprocessing and analysis

Key Insights
1. Customer Demographics & Revenue
Male customers generate higher revenue, with 3.9K male customers out of 16K total customers. Young customers contribute the majority of purchases, indicating strong engagement from this age group.
2. Product Performance
The average product rating is around 4, showing good customer satisfaction. The top 5 products based on sales are gloves, sandals, boots, hats, and skirts.
3. Customer Purchase Behavior
Customers tend to purchase products mainly when discounts are offered, indicating that pricing strategies strongly influence buying behavior.
4. Shipping Insights
Both Express and Standard shipping methods generate nearly equal revenue, suggesting customers are comfortable using either delivery option.
5. Subscription Feedback
Most customers are not satisfied with subscription offers, indicating an opportunity to improve subscription benefits and increase engagement.
